Put the site on your phone's home screen

On iPhone, in Safari: tap the Share button (the square with the arrow pointing up), then «Add to Home Screen».

On Android, in Chrome: tap the menu ⋮ at the top right, then «Install app» or «Add to Home screen».

The app opens full screen, like the others on your phone. The cards you follow stay in the browser where you added them: use «Save a backup» and «Load a backup» to move them.

All cards

Every card worth at least €1 on Cardmarket and how much its price has moved. Search, filter and sort as you like; press «Follow» to put a card in your list. «EX+ offers» opens Cardmarket with only the copies in Excellent condition or better: you need to be logged in to Cardmarket.

How much a deck costs

Paste a list: for every card the site finds its cheapest printing, normal or foil, and tells you where it costs less to buy it, on Cardmarket or on CardTrader. Gold-bordered, oversized and other non-tournament cards are left out.

Follow a whole set
The code is the abbreviation in brackets next to the set name when you search for a card. The group's cards go into the list under the group name: pick it in the menu above the list and sort by 7 or 30 days.
Or: every card above a price
It uses the normal (non-foil) price, printing by printing. Press the button again to include cards that have since risen above the threshold; those that fell below stay in the group, so you see the drops.
Or: the most expensive cards, one version per card
For each card it keeps only the most expensive version, normal or foil (for example a Secret Lair foil), in the format chosen above. It takes a few minutes: it checks every printing of about twice as many cards as requested.
Sets of groups (e.g. the whole meta)
Tick the groups to combine. A card in several decks appears only once. To change a set, save it again with the same name.
Alerts
Moves of less than €1 never trigger the percentage alert, so cheap cards don't flood you with alerts. Alerts arrive as notifications after each update if the browser allows it; otherwise you read them at the top. You set each card's target price in the list: you get an alert on the day the price drops below it. For the target price the site uses the higher of the trend and the cheapest copy on sale: so the alert goes off only when you can really buy below your price, and not because of a single damaged copy sold off cheaply.

Source: Cardmarket trend price for the chosen printing, via Scryfall, updated once a day. History starts on the day you add the card and is stored in this browser: clearing browsing data deletes it, so press «Save a backup» now and then. To move to another computer or browser, use «Load a backup».

How it works

A free Cardmarket price tracker for Magic: The Gathering. It runs in your browser, with no account and nothing to install.

  • Follow any card in any printing, normal or foil, and see how much its trend price rose or fell over 1, 7 and 30 days.
  • Import a whole deck (Commander, Duel Commander, Modern, Legacy) and find the most expensive version of every card, even full art, borderless or showcase foil only.
  • Follow a whole set, or every card above a price that is legal in your format, to spot the spikes and the deals.
  • Set a target price and get an alert when the card drops below it.

FAQ

Where do the prices come from?

From the Cardmarket trend price of each printing, published by Scryfall and updated once a day. This app is not affiliated with Cardmarket or Scryfall.

The «EX or better» prices come from the offers on CardTrader: the average of the 3 cheapest English copies in Mint, Near Mint or Slightly Played condition, updated once a day. This app is not affiliated with CardTrader.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser: nobody else sees it. Save a backup now and then so you don't lose the history.

Why don't I see any changes at first?

Changes show up from the next day, because the history starts on the day you add a card.
